# Color Routes Privacy Policy

**Publisher:** Coretech220  
**App:** Color Routes (`com.coretech.colorroutes`)  
**Effective date:** 28 October 2026  
**Contact:** coretech.com.support@gmail.com

## Who we are

Color Routes is an Android puzzle game published by Coretech220. This policy describes the game with package name com.coretech.colorroutes and the services it uses. For privacy questions, contact coretech.com.support@gmail.com.

You can read this policy at any time from Options → Privacy policy in the game. Advertising privacy choices, when Google makes them available for your device, are under Options → Privacy options.

## Game progress and local storage

The game saves puzzle progress, unfinished boards, settings, daily rewards, hint and Undo balances, and earned-ad reward receipts in app storage on your device. Color Routes does not require a game account and does not operate its own cloud-save service in this version.

If you choose to export a progress backup, Android lets you choose where to save or share that file. A backup may contain your game progress and preferences. Its destination and any cloud storage you select have their own privacy practices. Importing a backup replaces local progress after your confirmation.

Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it may remove local progress and inventory. A backup you exported remains at the location you chose until you delete it.

## Advertising and Google AdMob

Color Routes uses Google AdMob, Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on your location, device, settings, consent choices and Google’s configuration, these services may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, app interactions, advertising activity, and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and meet legal requirements, as described in its policies.

Rewarded ads are optional. When you have no available assistance, the game offers one hint or five Undos for the current level before you choose to watch an ad. A reward is earned when the advertising SDK confirms completion. Automatic ads may appear at breaks after eligible campaign levels. The no-ads purchase removes automatic between-level ads; optional rewarded ads remain available.

Where applicable, Google’s consent form explains advertising purposes and choices before ads are requested. In the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, review the form shown on your device. Use Privacy options in the game, when available, to review or change those choices. The ads available after a choice may differ; rejecting personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads.

You can also manage your Android advertising privacy controls in the device’s Settings. The exact controls and wording depend on your Android version. Color Routes does not sell your saved puzzle progress or run its own advertising-data marketplace.

## Purchases and Google Play

Optional in-app purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details. The game receives purchase information such as product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase status and purchase history needed to verify purchases, deliver inventory and restore eligible non-consumable purchases. Purchase records used by the game are kept in app-private storage.

Color Routes does not receive your full payment-card number. Google controls its own payment and transaction records, retention periods and refund processes.

## Permissions and device features

The game uses network access for advertising, consent and Google Play services. Its Android build includes advertising-ID and advertising-services permissions used by those services, along with system permissions for supported SDK operation. Vibration is optional and can be switched off in Options.

The game does not request access to your precise location, contacts, microphone, camera or photo library. Backup import and export use Android’s document picker for files you choose. Share opens Android’s chooser only after you tap Share; Rate us opens the game’s Google Play listing only after you choose that action.

## Sharing, service providers and international processing

Google acts according to its applicable terms and privacy policies for advertising, consent and purchases. Its services may process data in countries other than your own. Any storage provider or sharing application you select for a backup or Share action processes the information you send under its own policies.

This policy is hosted on GitHub. Visiting it is subject to GitHub’s privacy practices; GitHub may process technical request information such as IP address and browser details. Our policy files do not add analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies or a contact form.

## Retention and security

Local game information is retained until you remove it, replace it by importing a backup, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Files you export are retained separately until you delete them. Google and other services determine retention of information they process under their own policies.

The game uses app-private storage for native purchase and reward records and loads gameplay from bundled local assets. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and any exported backups protected.

## Your choices and privacy requests

You can change game settings in Options, review available advertising consent choices through Privacy options, manage Android advertising privacy settings, and remove local game data through Android Settings. Privacy policy remains available independently of whether Google requires a consent form for your device.

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or withdrawal of consent. For questions about Color Routes, contact coretech.com.support@gmail.com. We do not hold a separate gameplay account that lets us retrieve local progress from your device. For information held by Google, use Google’s privacy controls and request channels. These choices do not affect legal rights you may have to contact a data-protection authority.

## Parents and guardians

If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child’s use of Color Routes or information processed through its services, contact coretech.com.support@gmail.com. Do not send sensitive information about a child in a public message. Parents and guardians can use Android and Google Play controls to manage access to apps and purchases. Contact us if you believe personal information about a child has been collected through Color Routes.
